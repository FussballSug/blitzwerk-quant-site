03

THREE AI RESEARCH TRADERS

Independent strategies.
Shared controls.

Implemented paper workflow: these software trader roles evaluate candidates from a screened universe of eligible US stocks. The universe first filters for tradability, liquidity, spread, price, data quality, volatility, and risk. Each trader then applies its quantitative strategy, and the desk ranks the candidates using out-of-sample evidence and estimated trading costs. A shared portfolio controller resolves competing proposals and enforces deterministic account-wide controls before any paper submission. Research validation remains a separate qualification track. No trade is proposed when no candidate qualifies. Strategy assignments are permanent; individual stock symbols can change. Paper operation tests the machinery; it does not establish a profitable edge or authorize live trading.
