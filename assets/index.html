THREE AI RESEARCH TRADERS

Independent strategies.
Shared controls.

PAPER / SIMULATED: these software trader roles are assigned to scan a screened universe of eligible US stocks. The universe first filters for tradability, liquidity, spread, price, data quality, volatility, and risk. Each trader then applies its quantitative strategy, and the desk ranks the candidates using out-of-sample evidence and estimated trading costs. The highest-ranked qualified opportunity can advance through independent validation and deterministic risk controls. No trade is proposed when no candidate qualifies. Strategy assignments are permanent; individual stock symbols can change. Current task and operating evidence appear below; missing metrics and strategy versions remain unknown.
